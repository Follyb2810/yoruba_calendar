# 256 Odu Ifa Collection

This document compiles the complete list of the 256 Odu Ifa, based on traditional Yoruba Ifa divination system. The Odu are organized in order of seniority, starting with the 16 principal (Meji) Odu, followed by their 240 combinations. Each Odu represents a unique energy pattern, with associated verses (ese Ifa), parables, proverbs, prescriptions, and metaphysical meanings derived from Ifa oral tradition.

**Important Notes:**

- The full corpus of Ifa verses is vast—each Odu can have 600–800 or more verses (ese Ifa), making the total collection enormous (over 150,000 verses). These are traditionally transmitted orally by trained Babalawos (Ifa priests) and are not fully documented in a single free online source due to their sacred nature and variations by lineage.
- For the 16 principal Odu (Meji), I've included brief metaphysical principles and explanations based on traditional interpretations.
- For the combined Odu, explanations are generally a blend of the two principal Odu involved (e.g., Ogbe-Oyeku combines light/expansion with darkness/contraction, often signifying transitions or balance between life and death). Detailed verses require consultation with an Ifa practitioner or specialized books (e.g., "The Holy Odu" by Awo Fategbe Fatunmbi or the series by Marcelo Madan).
- This is for educational and cultural purposes. Ifa is a living tradition; interpretations can vary. For personal divination or rituals, seek a qualified Babalawo.
- Sources: Compiled from traditional listings (e.g., Pressbooks.pub African Traditional Religions Textbook, Nairaland forums, and Ifa lineage teachings).

## The 16 Principal Odu (Meji) with Explanations

These are the foundational "double" Odu, where both legs are identical. They are the most senior.

1. **Ogbe Meji (Ejiogbe)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Supporter – manifestation of pure light and expansion from source outward.
   - **Explanation**: Represents new beginnings, success, victory, and clarity. It advises on growth, protection, and aligning with divine light. Common themes: prosperity, guidance from Orunmila, and avoiding haste.

2. **Oyeku Meji (Ejioyeku)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Averter of Death – manifestation of pure darkness and contraction to the ancestral world.
   - **Explanation**: Deals with endings, death, ancestors, and avoidance of misfortune. It emphasizes patience, sacrifice to Egungun (ancestors), and transforming negativity into peace.

3. **Iwori Meji (Ejiwori)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Deep Seer – manifestation of pure thought and projection into the astral realm.
   - **Explanation**: Focuses on inner vision, intelligence, and spiritual insight. Advises on meditation, avoiding deception, and using wisdom to overcome challenges.

4. **Odi Meji (Ejiodi, Idi Meji)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Seal – manifestation of coolness, forming structure from chaos.
   - **Explanation**: Symbolizes foundations, marriage, fertility, and stability. It warns against betrayal and prescribes rituals for protection and family harmony.

5. **Irosun Meji (Ejiirosun)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Resounding Osun – manifestation of mystical vision and seeing the invisible.
   - **Explanation**: Relates to intuition, Osun (river goddess), and hidden knowledge. Themes include healing, beauty, and caution against envy or spiritual attacks.

6. **Owonrin Meji (Ejiowonrin, Ohenren Meji)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Reversed Head – manifestation of transformation and ending cycles.
   - **Explanation**: Indicates change, reversal of fortunes, and adaptability. Advises on flexibility, ebo (sacrifice) for positive shifts, and avoiding rigidity.

7. **Obara Meji (Eji Obara)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Resting and Hovering One – manifestation of creative power and manifesting desires.
   - **Explanation**: Represents wealth, leadership, and manifestation. It encourages hard work, honesty, and rituals for abundance.

8. **Okanran Meji (Ejiokanran)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Beater of Sticks – manifestation of joy and happiness.
   - **Explanation**: Deals with celebration, community, and resolving conflicts. Warns against gossip and prescribes unity and gratitude.

9. **Ogunda Meji (Ejiogunda)**
   - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Creator – manifestation of determination and perseverance.
   - **Explanation**: Symbolizes strength, warfare (Ogun), and creation. Advises on courage, tools/weapons, and overcoming obstacles through effort.

10. **Osa Meji (Ejiosa)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Runaway – manifestation of random chance and unpredictability.
    - **Explanation**: Relates to escape, witchcraft, and sudden events. Emphasizes protection from enemies and adaptability to chaos.

11. **Ika Meji (Ejika)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Controller – manifestation of balance between opposing forces.
    - **Explanation**: Focuses on control, justice, and harmony. Warns against wickedness and advises ethical behavior and equilibrium.

12. **Oturupon Meji (Ejioturupon)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Bearer – manifestation of endurance and bearing burdens.
    - **Explanation**: Deals with hardship, health, and resilience. Prescribes patience and sacrifices for long-term stability.

13. **Otura Meji (Ejiotura, Etura Meji)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Comforter/Disrupter – manifestation of peace and tranquility.
    - **Explanation**: Represents beauty, Irunmole (deities), and calm. Advises on aesthetics, relationships, and avoiding disruption.

14. **Irete Meji (Ejirete)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Crusher – manifestation of victory and success.
    - **Explanation**: Symbolizes triumph, pressing forward, and achievement. Encourages persistence and rituals for conquest.

15. **Ose Meji (Ejiose)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Conqueror – manifestation of will and assertion.
    - **Explanation**: Focuses on power, conquest, and Osanyin (herbalism). Warns against abuse of power and advises strategic action.

16. **Ofun Meji (Ejiofun, Oragun Meji)**
    - **Metaphysical Principle**: The Giver – manifestation of abundance and prosperity.
    - **Explanation**: Represents giving, whiteness (purity), and wealth. Emphasizes generosity, cleanliness, and alignment with Olodumare.

### The 16 Principal Odu (Meji)

1. **Ogbe Meji (Ejiogbe, The Supporter)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Pure light, expansion, open road, alignment with destiny (atunwa/reincarnation). Harmony brings abundance; misalignment causes poverty/illness. Avoid arrogance.
   - **Key Phrase**: Ogbe creates an open road.
   - **Oriki (Invocation)**: Ejiogbe, Ejiogbe, Ejiogbe. Mo be yin, kiegbe mi ki’mi niyi, ki e egbe mi ki’mi n’ola... (Beg for honor, respect, victory over enemies).
   - **Sample Verses (Ese Ifa)**:
     1. "Ogbe ká relé Ogbé ká relé / Omọ Osín / Ogbé ká relé Ogbé ká relé / Omọ Orá..." (Parable: Cast for the Farmer and Hunter; they offered sacrifice and found good tidings. Explanation: Combine efforts for better life; good fortune returns).
     2. "There is no place on earth I can go where I will not meet happiness" (Cast for Odunkun; made ebo for sweeter life. Parable: Sweet potato journey leads to joy. Explanation: Blessings of abundance; act calmly).
     3. "QrQ tí bá Íl dunni lárankán eni / Ire omo ní bá ñ dün wá..." (Parable: Orunmila saddened by incidents; offered sacrifice for honesty. Explanation: Be upright; truth prevents disarray).
     4. "Ogbé wéyin wo Bájáá réó baá pa ikún..." (Cast for Oñyagbé; offered sacrifice for farming success. Parable: Hunter and farmer unite. Explanation: Good behavior leads to friendship; avoid stinginess).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Come and meet us with good tidings." (Ethical: Insist on justice; rise above enemies).
   - **Prescriptions (Etutu)**: 4 pigeons, 4 roosters, palm oil, corn cakes; offer to Obatala/Ogun. For children: 4 hens, rat, catfish; to Ibeji.
   - **Taboos (Eewo)**: Groundnuts, mushrooms, black cloths.

2. **Oyeku Meji (Ejioyeku, The Averter of Death)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Darkness, contraction, end of cycles (e.g., death to ancestor realm). Peace in maturity; premature end negative.
   - **Key Phrase**: Oyeku creates an end to a cycle.
   - **Oriki**: Oyeku Meji, Oyeku Meji... Mo be yin, bi iku ba sunmo... (Avert death, protect children).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "Joy received at home is not as strong as joy received on the farm" (Cast for Onikabidun; doubled joy with hoes. Explanation: Persevere in upbringing; train children).
     2. "My right hand Oye my left hand Oye-two Oye become true..." (Cast for Ape; made ebo to avert loss. Explanation: Peace; don't lose gains).
     3. "Akogi l’apa tied himself with a rope" (Cast for slanderer; Orunmila victorious. Explanation: Worship Ifa; justice).
     4. "Whatever one feels sad about is one's sore point" (Explanation: Honest living; few upright people).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Train children in the way they should go" (Biblical parallel).
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, 4 hens, rat, catfish; to Ibeji. Mark 5 hoes with iyerosun.
   - **Taboos**: Grey rat; no leaf cover in rain.

3. **Iwori Meji (Ejiwori, The Deep Seer)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Consciousness, transformation via passion/conflict. Growth or defeat; drive to procreate.
   - **Key Phrase**: Iwori creates transformation.
   - **Oriki**: Iwori Meji... Mo be yin ki a ffoju re wo mi... (Good eyes, fertility).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "The mortar that we use to pound yam is not used to pierce elu" (Cast for Olu; won ayo games. Explanation: Accept advice; skill in chance).
     2. "What you like I don’t like which one should be among us" (Cast for Onimuti Iwori children; protected with Ibeji. Explanation: Avert disease).
     3. "Two bottoms that I use to sit on give me comfort" (Cast for Onibode; goodness twice a day. Explanation: Prayer manifests dreams).
     4. "Eetalewa with gbagbdegbagada eyes" (Cast for Orunmila; passed unharmed. Explanation: Avert misfortune).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Hear counsel to be wise" (African proverb).
   - **Prescriptions**: 1 pigeon, 1 hen, corn cakes; to Obatala. Sheep to Eleda.
   - **Taboos**: Dog, ori fruit.

4. **Odi Meji (Ejiodi, The Seal)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Rebirth, new forms from old. Hold on past negative.
   - **Key Phrase**: Odi creates rebirth.
   - **Oriki**: Odi Meji... Mo be yin, kie bami di ona ofo... (Close loss, avert epidemic).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "Ogbe di kaka / Ogbe di kunkun..." (Cast for Esu Odara; made ebo. Explanation: Beware betrayal; take time to understand).
     2. "Grave tragedy in dying young" (Pray for long life. Explanation: Fear of Lord prolongs days).
     3. "Two bottoms that I use to sit on give me comfort" (Blessings slip away without ebo).
     4. "Eetalewa with gbagbdegbagada eyes" (Avert stones/disease).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Pour libations for parents" (Husia-Khemet).
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, edibles; to Esu. 3 stones, 3 pigeons; to Esu.
   - **Taboos**: Yam breaking; no digging near entrance.

5. **Irosun Meji (Ejiirosun, The Resounding Osun)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Menstrual blood, ancestral lineage, potential fulfillment. Resistance negative.
   - **Key Phrase**: Irosun creates fulfillment of potential.
   - **Oriki**: Irosun Meji... Mo be yin, kie jeki awon omo-araye gburo... (World hears of riches).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "Their mouth their mouth" (Cast for Apeni; protected. Explanation: Enemies).
     2. "Porogun of igbodu" (Cast for Okansusu; children ate food. Explanation: Blessing of children).
     3. "Seek honor; become revered ancestor" (Teach children).
     4. "The front wheel bears money" (Enemies change hearts).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Advise; adversity teaches."
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, 4 hens; to Ori/Egungun. Yiyan, eba; to Ori.
   - **Taboos**: Snake, red cloths.

6. **Owonrin Meji (Ejiowonrin, The Reversed Head)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Chaos, unexpected change. Deal positively.
   - **Key Phrase**: Owonrin creates unexpected change.
   - **Oriki**: Owonrin Meji... Mo be yin, ki eso ibi de rere... (Turn evil to good).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "Thief but not a thief" (Cast for Owon; became chief. Explanation: Fame/title).
     2. "It is a big tree" (Cast for Ologbo; traveled unharmed. Explanation: Avoid hexes).
     3. "Be told and accept advice" (Know the world).
     4. "The hard wood of the forest" (Bring rain).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Hear counsel to be wise."
   - **Prescriptions**: 6 pigeons, 6 hens; to Obatala/Esu. 10 rats, palm oil; to Obatala.
   - **Taboos**: Not specified.

7. **Obara Meji (Eji Obara, The Resting and Hovering One)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Inner transformation, strength vs. egotism. Humility foundation.
   - **Key Phrase**: Obara creates inner transformation.
   - **Oriki**: Obara Meji... Mo be yin, ki e si’na aje fun me... (Wealth ways).
   - **Sample Verses**:
     1. "Washing the right hand" (Cast for Awun; good head. Explanation: Clean head).
     2. "Abarere Awo Odan" (Cast for Odan; shade. Explanation: New projects).
     3. "Uplift from poverty; be industrious" (Righteousness overcomes).
     4. "No wise man can tie water" (Poetic lines).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Avoid sleep to avoid poverty."
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, 4 hens; to Esu.
   - **Taboos**: Hunting small birds.

8. **Okanran Meji (Ejiokanran, The Beater of Sticks)**
   - **Metaphysical Meaning**: Balance head/heart, humility. Emotional change negative.
   - **Key Phrase**: Okanran creates new possibilities.
   - **Oriki**: Okanran Meji... Mo be yin, ki e jeki oran ibanje maa kan... (Enemies meet adversity).
   - **Sample Verses**:

     2. "There are different ways" (Cast for Igbegbe; clear vision. Explanation: Clear confusion).
     3. "Stubbornness harms; listen to truth" (Patience good character).
     4. "Subtlety better than force" (African proverb).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Patience brings good character."
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, 4 hens, bean cakes; to Ogun/Esu.
   - **Taboos**: Not specified.

9. **Ogunda Meji (Ejiogunda, The Creator)**

   - **Oriki**: Ogunda Meji... Mo be yin, kiedai ni’de Arun... (Release from misfortune).

     4. "Porongodo Ségì sóórúpa" (Heavy burden).
   - **Parables/Proverbs**: "Gentleness praises the wise."
   - **Prescriptions**: 4 pigeons, 4 hens, leather; to Ogun/Ifa. 2 bells, 2 pigeons; to diviner.
   - **Taboos**: Carry money protected.
